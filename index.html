BEHIND THE DOODLE

The crowd is the controller.

This experiment asks how an atmosphere can change someone’s willingness to try. It’s an expressive model, not a prediction of real people.

Try a small experiment

Cheer at full intensity, then switch to heckling. Watch confidence, posture, speed and jump height change. Try quiet next: the skater slowly returns to a calm baseline.

How the code works

Button events set a mood. The slider adds up to 40 people and increases their influence and sound. Click Enable sound for synthesized cheers, claps, low heckles, or a quiet rustle. Each animation frame moves confidence toward a target: cheering up to 95%, quiet at 55%, and heckling down to 10%. Conditions choose a feeling and caption. While cheering, the skater changes pose and movement only at 60%, 80%, and 100% crowd intensity: ollie, grab, and kickflip. Below 60%, or in Quiet and Heckle modes, the skater uses an easy riding pose. Confidence still responds gradually. The soundtrack layers rhythmic claps, whoops, and a pentatonic melody.
